A studio of dedicated craftspeople.

Founded in 2019, TechHub began as a small team in Peshawar with one clear ambition — to build digital products that perform at the highest possible level and create real business impact.

Five years on, we are a 45-person studio serving clients from early-stage startups to Fortune 500 enterprises across 30+ countries. Our work has helped companies raise over $50M in funding and reach millions of users worldwide.

We don't just write code. We partner with you, understand your constraints, and engineer systems built to grow alongside your ambitions.
